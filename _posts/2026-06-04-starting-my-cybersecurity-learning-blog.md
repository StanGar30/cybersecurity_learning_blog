# Starting My Cybersecurity Learning Blog

Today I finally decided to start documenting my cybersecurity learning journey.

I have already been studying for some time before creating this blog, so I will not try to recreate every step I have taken so far. Instead, this blog will focus on my progress from this point forward.

## Current Learning Resources

At the moment I am learning through multiple platforms:

* TryHackMe (THM) - beginner-friendly platform with structured learning paths.
* Hack The Box (HTB) - more hands-on and practical exercises with real-world scenarios.
* My own training platform and lab environment.

## Current Focus

My main goal is to become a Junior Web Penetration Tester and Security Researcher.

At the moment I am focusing on:

* Web application security
* Networking fundamentals
* Linux
* Vulnerability discovery
* Practical hands-on exercises

## Why I Started This Blog

I want to document what I learn, track my progress, and create a public record of my development.

I also hope that future beginners may find some of my notes, mistakes, and lessons useful.

## Next Steps

In future posts I plan to share:

* Learning notes
* Interesting findings
* Lab write-ups
* Challenges I encounter
* Progress updates

Let's see where this journey leads.
