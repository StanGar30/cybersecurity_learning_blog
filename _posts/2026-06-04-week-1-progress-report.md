## What I Learned

Most of the TryHackMe modules were focused on building a strong theoretical foundation. Topics included networking concepts, web technologies, operating systems, and basic cybersecurity terminology.

Although some of the material was already familiar to me, it was useful to refresh my knowledge and fill a few gaps.

Hack The Box felt significantly more practical. Instead of reading theory, I spent most of the time interacting with a Linux machine, executing commands, navigating the file system, and solving tasks directly in the environment.

## Challenges

One of the most challenging parts this week was working with Linux file management.

Finding files, creating directories, modifying files, and navigating the filesystem efficiently required more practice than I initially expected.

## Interesting Findings

The most interesting topic for me on TryHackMe was HTML Injection. While it is a relatively basic concept, it was interesting to see how simple input handling mistakes can lead to unexpected behavior.

On Hack The Box, I noticed that some tasks intentionally require researching information outside of the provided material. I believe this is a useful approach because it encourages independent problem-solving and information gathering.
